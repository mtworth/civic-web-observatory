This is where we'll publish hand-written analysis and thought leadership on the state of the American civic web — HTTPS adoption, hosting concentration, accessibility gaps, and whatever else the dataset surfaces that's worth a closer look.

Unlike the Insights dashboard, which is generated directly from the live dataset, reports here are written by a person, with context and interpretation the raw numbers can't provide on their own.

Check back as the dataset grows.
